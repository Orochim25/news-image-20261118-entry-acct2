One image that sums up the world's news of Wednesday, 18 November 2026:
30 stories across 7 regions, drawn as a newspaper front page.

- 10 illustrated stories: Kuwait and Bahrain intercept Iranian drones,
  Xi greets Trump as APEC opens in Shenzhen, COP31 splits over a
  fossil-fuel phase-out, the Gaza truce warning, Bangladesh wage protests,
  Voyager 1 one light-day from Earth, the Haiti raid, Russian strikes in
  Ukraine, Ethiopia and Eritrea massing troops, and GTA VI going on sale.
- 20 more briefs, plus a world map showing where it happened.
- Every story here happened or was first reported on Wednesday,
  18 November 2026. Sources: Reuters, AP, AFP, Al Jazeera, Bloomberg.

## Entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to the output directory. It runs twice and writes identical
bytes both times. `entry.png` is a 1024×1024 PNG.
